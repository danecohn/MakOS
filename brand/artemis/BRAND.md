## Identity

An original crescent moon, drawn bow, eastward arrow, and guiding star: a reference to Artemis, the Greek goddess associated with the moon, wilderness, and archery. The crescent expresses a calm home for software; the arrow expresses agency and forward motion. Use the simple mark at small sizes and the complete wordmark for publisher credits.

## Moonrise visual system

Companion software that feels at home on a Mac, thoughtfully available for Windows and Linux. Original artwork and system typography; no proprietary Apple artwork, fonts, sounds, logos, or public Apple operating-system theme names.

- Pearl `#f5f0e6`: warm canvas, gentle highlights.
- Moonlight `#dce8e5`: translucent silver glass and borders.
- Coastal ink `#273941`: typography and dark surfaces.
- Harbor blue `#526e7b`: accessible interactive emphasis.
- Warm brass `#b89463`: illustration and restrained decorative details; use darker brass `#86623b` for text on pearl.
- Night sea `#14252f`: dark appearance.

Use soft layered depth, subtle gradients, fine borders, generous breathing room, readable controls, and tactile but restrained surfaces. Keep controls keyboard accessible, respect reduced motion, and use layouts designed for touch. Light and dark appearances share the same structure. Avoid neon, black-and-lime automotive dashboards, and exaggerated motion.

## Asset provenance

Identity PNGs, album artwork, and editable `.asprite.json` projects were authored and exported through **AltStack Sprite's native document/compositing/PNG engine**. SVG masters are original matching vector artwork. Songs were composed through **AltStack Box's song tools and rendered with its real BeepBox synth**, not an unrelated music generator. Editable `.song.json` projects accompany each track. Audio exports use Box's native WAV encoder and its bundled lamejs MP3 encoder.

Box's underlying synth is BeepBox by John Nesky and contributing authors, MIT licensed; lamejs is LGPL licensed. Those tool licenses and third-party acknowledgments remain intact in their repositories. Original 2026 brand artwork and compositions are credited to Artemis Softworks LLC. Code remains under each repository's existing license. Brand assets identify the publisher and do not grant permission to impersonate it.
