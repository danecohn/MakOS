# AltStack sculpted product icons

Original product artwork for Artemis Softworks LLC. The AltStack suite icon incorporates the Artemis crescent-and-bow identity; product names and application identifiers stay compatible.

The new raster icons were generated and refined with the built-in image-generation tool, then exported to platform PNG, WebP, ICO and ICNS formats. The vector AltStack stack-and-Artemis mark and wordmark are authored SVG. The separate Artemis publisher artwork and Lunar Companion album retain their original Sprite/Box provenance.

Publisher, developer and original artwork credit: Artemis Softworks LLC. Founder: Demetria Althea Cohn. No Apple artwork or proprietary fonts are redistributed. Upstream product and software licenses remain with their authors.
